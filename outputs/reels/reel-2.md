# Reel 2: The Menu Magic

**Hook:** Turn your paper menu or price list into an online shop in seconds!  
**Duration:** Exactly 25 Seconds  
**Format:** 9:16 Vertical (Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts)  
**Tone:** Fun, effortless, and amazed  
**Audience:** Cafe owners, restaurants, bakeries, grocery stores, and salons  

---

## The 25-Second Script

| Time | Visual on Screen | Big Text on Screen | Voiceover (Spoken Word) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 – 0:04** (4s) | A busy cafe owner looking at a printed paper menu, shaking their head at the thought of typing it all out. | Stop typing 50 items by hand! | *"Still typing your entire shop menu item by item on your phone?"* |
| **0:04 – 0:10** (6s) | Screen shows meelTe. Tap **Multiple Items (Shop)**, then tap the magic star button: **Import from Photos**. | Tap `+` ➔ Import from Photos | *"Stop! In meelTe, choose Shop Ad and tap 'Import from Photos'."* |
| **0:10 – 0:17** (7s) | Snapping two quick photos of the printed menu. A bright sparkle effect sweeps across, and neat product cards with prices instantly appear! | AI reads names & prices! | *"Just snap a photo of your paper menu or price board. meelTe automatically reads the items and prices for you!"* |
| **0:17 – 0:21** (4s) | Scrolling through the neat catalog of up to 100 items. Tapping **Create Advertisement**—a mini digital store appears live in the neighborhood feed. | Digital shop live in 1 tap! | *"Review your items, pin your location, and publish up to a hundred products in one tap."* |
| **0:21 – 0:25** (4s) | Warm brand end-card with the meelTe logo and vibrant colors. | **meelTe**<br>Open your shop today | *"Turn paper into customers. Start selling on meelTe free."* |

---

## Creative Guide
* **Brand Colors:** Warm lavender background (`#F4EFFD`) for clean contrast, brand purple (`#743BAA`) for headlines, warm orange (`#FEA711`) for the AI magic badge.
* **Music:** Playful, uplifting whistle or synth-pop.
* **Sound Effects (SFX):**
  * *Camera shutter* (0:09)
  * *Magic sparkle / swoosh sound* as the product cards auto-fill (0:13)
  * *Success ding* when the store goes live (0:20)
