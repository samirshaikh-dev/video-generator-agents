# Reel 3: Walking Distance Deals

**Hook:** What amazing local deals are hiding right down your street?  
**Duration:** Exactly 25 Seconds  
**Format:** 9:16 Vertical (Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts)  
**Tone:** Curious, upbeat, and community-first  
**Audience:** Everyday shoppers, foodies, neighbors looking for local discounts  

---

## The 25-Second Script

| Time | Visual on Screen | Big Text on Screen | Voiceover (Spoken Word) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 – 0:04** (4s) | A person walking through a lively neighborhood street, glancing at their phone with a curious smile. | Deals right around the corner? | *"Ever wonder what secret discounts are happening right around your corner?"* |
| **0:04 – 0:10** (6s) | Opening the meelTe app. Tapping the megaphone **Advertisements** tab. Deals appear ordered by distance (e.g., 200m away, 450m away). | Nearby deals, closest first! | *"Open meelTe and tap Advertisements. You’ll see real offers from local shops, sorted by who’s closest to you."* |
| **0:10 – 0:16** (6s) | Tapping a mouthwatering bakery ad showing a `30% OFF` badge. Double-tapping the photo to send a heart like. | Fresh offers from real shops | *"Fresh bread, boutique sales, or cafe combos—tap any ad to see real prices and customer photos."* |
| **0:16 – 0:21** (5s) | Tapping the blue **Directions** button. Google Maps smoothly opens, showing a 3-minute walking route directly to the store door. | 1-Tap Directions on Google Maps | *"Love what you see? Tap Directions and walk straight to their door!"* |
| **0:21 – 0:25** (4s) | Brand outro card with friendly community illustrations. | **meelTe**<br>Discover your neighborhood | *"Support local and save money. Discover your neighborhood on meelTe!"* |

---

## Creative Guide
* **Brand Colors:** Deep Purple (`#743BAA`) for clean text banners, Soft Lavender (`#F4EFFD`) for the card backgrounds, Orange (`#FEA711`) for the distance badge and heart icon.
* **Music:** Cheerful, breezy indie pop / sunny walk music.
* **Sound Effects (SFX):**
  * *Friendly phone unlock chime* (0:04)
  * *Heart pop sound* when double-tapping to like (0:14)
  * *Map 'whoosh' / pin drop* when Directions is tapped (0:18)
