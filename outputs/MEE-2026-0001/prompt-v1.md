## 1. Brief Summary

* **Objective:** Show local retail owners and merchants how easy and fast it is to create a Single Item deal with instant discount badges and publish it directly to nearby shoppers on meelTe.
* **Target Audience:** Local small-business owners, retail shopkeepers, bakery owners, and boutique managers.
* **Key Message:** Post your daily deal in under a minute and reach local customers directly with zero listing fees.
* **Brand Tone:** Playful, approachable, modern, friendly, and practical.

## 4. Time-Coded Shot Plan

### Shot 1: The Hook (0:00 – 4:00 | 120 frames)
* **Subject & Setting:** Welcoming neighborhood boutique/bakery. A friendly 2D vector shopkeeper with an expressive face wearing a purple apron with orange trim stands behind a warm wooden counter.
* **Action:** The shopkeeper holds up a featured product with an inviting smile, sets it down on the counter, and gestures enthusiastically toward the camera.
* **Camera:** Eye-level medium shot, subtle 3% push-in.
* **Transition:** Smooth cut to the mobile device in hand.

### Shot 2: The Plus Action (4:00 – 9:00 | 150 frames)
* **Subject & Setting:** First-person view of the shopkeeper's hand holding a smartphone with the clean meelTe app open.
* **Action:** Thumb taps the purple floating `+` button in the bottom right. A clean bottom sheet slides up; thumb selects "Single Item Advertisement". The camera viewfinder opens and snaps a crisp photo of the product.
* **Camera:** Close-up angled over-the-shoulder UI view; slight responsive micro-tilt on tap.
* **Transition:** Fast UI slide into the creation form.

### Shot 3: Discount & Map Pinning (9:00 – 16:00 | 210 frames)
* **Subject & Setting:** The meelTe ad creation form.
* **Action:** Form auto-fills original and discounted price. User selects the "Flat %" discount toggle. A vibrant orange badge reading `FLAT 25% OFF` pops onto the top corner of the product photo with a bouncy spring animation. The mini Google Map card pins the shop's storefront address with an orange marker.
* **Camera:** Direct UI focus scrolling smoothly down the form card.
* **Transition:** Micro-zoom toward the create button.

### Shot 4: Instant Live Activation (16:00 – 21:00 | 150 frames)
* **Subject & Setting:** Creation completion and immediate community feed.
* **Action:** Finger taps "Create Advertisement". A bright green notification bar slides in: *"Advertisement created"*. The app transitions directly into the community feed where the ad is top-ranked with a `50m away` proximity tag. Brief cut to an everyday shopper walking on the sidewalk outside looking down at their phone with a pleased expression.
* **Camera:** Fast split transition from UI confirmation to medium street view.
* **Transition:** Smooth dissolve into the brand closing card.

### Shot 5: Outro & Call to Action (21:00 – 25:00 | 120 frames)
* **Subject & Setting:** Brand closing card on a solid deep purple background (`#743BAA`) with a subtle curved lavender arc.
* **Action:** Official meelTe reverse-white wordmark scales in smoothly. Bold white headline: "Grow your store free" with a vibrant orange button "Try meelTe free".
* **Camera:** Centered static hold with kinetic text pop.
* **Transition:** Clean hold to final frame (750).

## 5. Provider-Neutral Main Prompt

```
Create a 25-second vertical 9:16 animated marketing video in a polished, playful 2D commercial vector illustration style. 

The video tells a continuous, 5-part visual story:
1. In a bright, welcoming local shop, a friendly illustrated shopkeeper wearing a deep purple apron (#743BAA) with warm orange accents (#FEA711) stands behind a wooden counter, proudly holding up a product and smiling at the viewer (0–4s).
2. The scene cuts to a clean first-person view of a smartphone running the meelTe app. A hand taps a purple circular '+' button at the bottom-right, selects 'Single Item Advertisement' from a modal, and the camera snaps a photo of the product (4–9s).
3. On the digital creation screen, the user enters pricing details and selects a discount toggle; a vibrant orange 'FLAT 25% OFF' badge springs onto the product photo, and an interactive mini map drops a location pin on the storefront (9–16s).
4. The user taps the purple 'Create Advertisement' button; an instant green banner confirms 'Advertisement created'. The ad appears live at the top of the local community feed with a '50m away' distance tag, and a neighbor on the sidewalk smiles as they see it on their phone (16–21s).
5. The video resolves onto a clean, solid deep purple (#743BAA) brand end-card featuring a centered reverse-white meelTe logo, bold white headline 'Grow your store free', and an orange CTA button 'Try meelTe free' (21–25s).

Maintain strict visual continuity: the shopkeeper, the product packaging, the phone UI, the store interior, and color palette remain identical across all cuts. Keep orange (#FEA711) and purple (#743BAA) as the dominant colors, supported by soft lavender (#F4EFFD) and charcoal (#171923). All character motion must be smooth, purposeful, and friendly. Do not use 3D, photorealism, clay textures, or visual clutter.
```
