# Reel 4: Zero Fees, Zero Waiting

**Hook:** Why pay hefty fees or wait days just to tell neighbors about your shop?  
**Duration:** Exactly 25 Seconds  
**Format:** 9:16 Vertical (Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts)  
**Tone:** Honest, empowering, and refreshing  
**Audience:** Small business owners, street vendors, home chefs, and freelancers  

---

## The 25-Second Script

| Time | Visual on Screen | Big Text on Screen | Voiceover (Spoken Word) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 – 0:04** (4s) | A shop owner looking frustrated at a calculator or a "Pending Approval" loading icon on a traditional ad app. | Tired of ad fees & waiting days? | *"Tired of expensive ad fees and waiting days for someone to approve your post?"* |
| **0:04 – 0:10** (6s) | Screen switches to a bright, clean meelTe screen. Fast, friendly taps filling in a store name and snapping a quick banner picture. | 100% Free • No Commissions | *"On meelTe, sharing your deals with your community is completely free. Zero charges, zero cuts."* |
| **0:10 – 0:16** (6s) | Finger taps the purple **Create Advertisement** button. Instantly, a bright green toast reads *"Advertisement created"*. | Instant Live • No Waiting Queue | *"And the best part? There’s no waiting queue. As soon as you hit create, your ad is live."* |
| **0:16 – 0:21** (5s) | Smiling customers walking into the physical store showing the ad on their phones. The owner waves happily. | Real customers at your counter | *"Nearby neighbors see your offers right away and walk straight into your store."* |
| **0:21 – 0:25** (4s) | Crisp brand outro card with meelTe logo and clear message. | **meelTe**<br>Grow your store free | *"No fees. No delays. Start promoting your store on meelTe today!"* |

---

## Creative Guide
* **Brand Colors:** Charcoal (`#171923`) for high-contrast headline, Orange (`#FEA711`) for the "100% Free" highlight, Purple (`#743BAA`) for brand buttons.
* **Music:** Upbeat, confident lo-fi groove or energetic claps and beat.
* **Sound Effects (SFX):**
  * *Buzzer sound* for old pending approval (0:02)
  * *Fresh snap / click* when tapping meelTe button (0:10)
  * *Cash register ding / bell* when customers walk in (0:18)
