# Reel 5: Schedule Your Weekend Rush

**Hook:** Planning a weekend sale? Set it up once and let it run automatically!  
**Duration:** Exactly 25 Seconds  
**Format:** 9:16 Vertical (Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts)  
**Tone:** Smart, calm, and organized  
**Audience:** Retailers, clothing stores, gift shops, and seasonal sellers  

---

## The 25-Second Script

| Time | Visual on Screen | Big Text on Screen | Voiceover (Spoken Word) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 – 0:04** (4s) | A shopkeeper hanging up a "Weekend Sale" poster, smiling and holding their smartphone. | Planning a weekend sale? | *"Planning a big weekend sale or holiday offer? Don't stress about posting on the day."* |
| **0:04 – 0:10** (6s) | Screen shows meelTe ad form. Finger taps the **Start Date** calendar picker and selects Friday. | Set start & end dates in advance | *"With meelTe, you can schedule your promotion ahead of time. Pick your start date and end date."* |
| **0:10 – 0:16** (6s) | The duration hint lights up: *"Runs for 3 days - until 11:59 PM Sunday"*. Finger taps **Create Advertisement**. | Wakes up automatically! | *"Your ad automatically wakes up at midnight when your sale begins, and wraps up when it ends."* |
| **0:16 – 0:21** (5s) | View of the owner enjoying a cup of coffee while customers browse the shelves on Saturday morning. | Set it once & relax | *"Set it, forget it, and let your neighborhood know exactly when the deals are hot."* |
| **0:21 – 0:25** (4s) | Brand outro card with meelTe logo and purple/orange styling. | **meelTe**<br>The smart partner for local shops | *"Plan your next sale with meelTe. Download and create today!"* |

## Creative Guide
* **Brand Colors:** Deep Purple (`#743BAA`) for calendars and structure, Orange (`#FEA711`) for the "Weekend Sale" highlight and active dates.
* **Music:** Smooth, relaxed, reassuring chill-hop or acoustic groove.
* **Sound Effects (SFX):**
  * *Calendar page flip sound* (0:07)
  * *Clock tick / chime* as midnight strikes (0:12)
  * *Gentle morning chime* (0:17)
