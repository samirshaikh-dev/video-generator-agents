# Reel 1: The 60-Second Flash Deal

**Hook:** Got one hot item or a quick deal you want to sell today?  
**Duration:** Exactly 25 Seconds  
**Format:** 9:16 Vertical (Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts)  
**Tone:** Energetic, friendly, and practical  
**Audience:** Boutique owners, retail shops, bakeries, and local sellers  

---

## The 25-Second Script

| Time | Visual on Screen | Big Text on Screen | Voiceover (Spoken Word) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 – 0:04** (4s) | A smiling local shopkeeper holding up a fresh product or pointing to a special shelf. | Got a deal to share today? | *"Got a product you want to sell fast today? Don't wait for foot traffic."* |
| **0:04 – 0:09** (5s) | Phone screen view: Tap the friendly purple **`+`** button and select **Single Item**. Take a quick photo of the product. | Tap `+` ➔ Single Item | *"Open meelTe, tap the plus sign, and snap a clear photo."* |
| **0:09 – 0:16** (7s) | Entering the price and tapping **Flat 25% OFF**. A bright orange discount badge pops onto the photo. Tap the map to pin the shop. | Pick your discount badge | *"Add your price, pick your discount badge, and pin your store on the map."* |
| **0:16 – 0:21** (5s) | Tapping the purple **Create Advertisement** button. A green checkmark appears: *"Advertisement created"*. Neighbors on the street see it pop up on their feeds. | Instantly live to nearby neighbors! | *"Hit create! No waiting for approvals—your deal goes live to nearby shoppers right now."* |
| **0:21 – 0:25** (4s) | Crisp meelTe brand outro in warm purple and bright orange with the brand logo. | **meelTe**<br>Grow your store free | *"Put your shop on the map. Try meelTe free today!"* |

---

## Creative Guide
* **Brand Colors:** Bright Orange accent (`#FEA711`) for the discount badge, Deep Purple (`#743BAA`) for buttons and headers.
* **Music:** Light, cheerful acoustic guitar or bouncy pop beat.
* **Sound Effects (SFX):**
  * *Camera shutter click* when taking photo (0:07)
  * *Pop sound* when the discount badge attaches (0:12)
  * *Gentle chime* when "Advertisement created" appears (0:18)
