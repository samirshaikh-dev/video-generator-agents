# Reel 6: Total Control In Your Pocket

**Hook:** Sold out of your special deal early? You have full control in seconds!  
**Duration:** Exactly 25 Seconds  
**Format:** 9:16 Vertical (Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts)  
**Tone:** Reassuring, flexible, and empowering  
**Audience:** Shop owners, bakers, restaurant managers who need on-the-fly flexibility

## Creative Guide
* **Brand Colors:** Lavender background (`#F4EFFD`) for clean readability, Charcoal (`#171923`) for high-contrast text, Purple (`#743BAA`) and Orange (`#FEA711`) for action badges.
* **Music:** Crisp, upbeat, modern pop rhythm.
* **Sound Effects (SFX):**
  * *Pencil scribble / edit tap sound* (0:12)
  * *Clean trash swoosh / clear chime* when deleted (0:19)
  * *Uplifting chord* on brand outro (0:22)
