# Copy Rules — meelTe Creative

## Hard limits

| Element | Limit |
|---|---|
| Headline | 6 words, 2 lines |
| Subhead (optional) | 8 words |
| Supporting line | 14 words |
| CTA | 4 words |
| Total words on creative | 26 |

Limits are ceilings, not targets. Most strong creatives use far fewer.

## Voice

- Second person or first person. "Your shop", "my shop" — not "businesses".
- Active voice. "Publish your catalogue", not "Catalogues can be published".
- Plain words. "Shop", not "retail establishment". "Orders", not "transactions".
- Warm, not cute. One light touch maximum per creative.
- Confident, not boastful.

## Claim discipline

Every one of these requires a supplied fact:

- numbers of any kind (customers, orders, growth, time saved)
- prices, discounts, percentages, "free"
- timeframes ("in 5 minutes", "same day")
- superlatives ("fastest", "best", "India's #1")
- guarantees
- comparisons to named or unnamed competitors
- testimonials and quotes

If the fact is not in the brief, the claim does not ship. Remove it and record it under `claims_removed` — do not soften it into vagueness.

## Language and locale

- Use the campaign's stated language. Default English for unspecified briefs.
- Indian English spelling for India-market campaigns: "catalogue", "colour", "organisation".
- Currency and number formats follow the market: ₹1,299 not INR 1299 for consumer-facing creatives.
- Do not machine-translate brand copy. Ask, or flag.

## Text that must never be generated by an image model

- the brand name "meelTe"
- prices and offer terms
- dates and deadlines
- legal or regulatory text
- any language other than the model's strongest

These are always overlaid deterministically in finishing.

## Common rewrites

| Weak | Better | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Introducing our new catalogue feature | Your shop, open all night | feature-led |
| Get started today | Publish your catalogue | vague CTA |
| Grow your business with meelTe | Sell more without hiring | abstract benefit |
| The all-in-one commerce solution | Your shop, online | jargon |
| Don't miss out! | (delete) | pressure without a deadline |
| 10x your sales | (remove) | unsupported claim |

## Punctuation and casing

- No full stop at the end of a headline.
- Sentence case for headlines and CTAs.
- Em dash for a beat: "Publish once — sell all day."
- No exclamation marks. One is permitted only in a genuine celebratory campaign.
- No ellipses.
- No ALL CAPS beyond three words.

## Multilingual creatives

If the campaign runs in more than one language:

1. Keep the visual layout identical.
2. Allow the headline to change length by up to 20%; re-fit the text box, do not shrink the type below the band.
3. Re-check line breaks per language — never inherit the English break points.
4. Do not reuse the English CTA as a placeholder.

## Failure signals

- Headline contains a number — check the source immediately.
- Copy uses "solution", "platform", "leverage", "seamless" — jargon.
- CTA says "Learn more" — not specific.
- Text is cramped or shrunk — copy exceeded the limit, cut words instead.
- Translated copy has odd line breaks — inherited English breaks.
